DBM-CoA AutoLearner v3 experimental. Automatically records instances, learns NPC and spell IDs, and attempts to share compact discoveries over DBMCoASync via SendAddonMessage CHANNEL. This requires client support and other players running v3. No raw combat logs or character names are sent. The draggable status indicator shows local events, discoveries, sent and received counts. Commands: /dbmcoa status, share on/off, ui on/off, channel on/off, save, clear. CAUTION: automatic joining of custom channels may change numbered chat channel assignments; client behavior cannot be guaranteed. If channel order matters, /dbmcoa channel off and /leave DBMCoASync. No timers are automatically generated.
